🏍️🇵🇰 How to Play Pak Ride 3D

Bike Mode

  • Tap ENTER BIKE to switch to the bike.
  • Use ◀ and ▶ to steer.
  • Hold BOOST for extra speed.
  • Avoid traffic and keep riding to increase your distance.

Walk Mode

  • Tap ENTER BIKE again to switch back to walking.
  • Use ◀ and ▶ to move around.

Mobile Controls

The game is designed for mobile touch controls. Landscape mode can make the game easier to play.
